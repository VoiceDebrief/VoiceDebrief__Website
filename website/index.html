Why this exists

General assistants can read your voice note. They just can't always transcribe it.

In our tests, a mainstream AI assistant identified a genuine WhatsApp voice note perfectly — codec, container, duration, sample rate — and still answered that it had no speech recognition available in that session. You can't predict when it will work. We do one job, and it works every time.

Made for WhatsApp audio

Voice notes are Opus audio in an Ogg container — and sometimes AAC when forwarded between iPhone apps. We accept both and detect the format from the file's content, never the extension.

One complete job

Not a step in someone else's workflow. Upload a voice note, get everything you need back at once: the words, the meaning, the summary — and a visual if you want it.

Nothing to install, nothing stored

Everything runs in your browser or app. There is no backend holding your audio — your files and your credits stay with you.

Privacy

Privacy is a mode you choose, not a disclaimer you scroll past

Different voice notes deserve different handling. Pick per upload:

ModeWhere it runsPrivacyPrice
ROUTED — DEFAULT Any provider via our model router No guarantee which provider processes it — we say so up front Cheapest
RESTRICTED Only providers you choose Named processors Higher
BROWSER-LOCAL Your own device Nothing leaves your device Possibly free
Honesty note: the default mode is the least private — requests are routed to whichever model provider serves them, and we cannot promise which one. If that matters for a recording, use restricted or browser-local mode. A full privacy notice ships with the beta.
