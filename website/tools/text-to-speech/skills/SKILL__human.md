# Text to speech — for people

Paste text, pick a voice, press the button, listen. If it is any good, download
the `.wav`.

- **You need an OpenRouter key** (`sk-or-v1-…`, from openrouter.ai/keys). It is
  kept in this browser only, sent only to OpenRouter, and billed to you. A short
  read costs a fraction of a penny — the tool shows what it actually cost.
- **Nothing is uploaded to us.** There is no backend: the audio is made in your
  browser and stays there until you download it.
- **The voice reads your words verbatim.** Steer the delivery with the script
  itself: a short lead-in, ordinary punctuation, sentences you would say out
  loud. `onyx` and `echo` read like a news anchor; `nova` and `shimmer` are
  lighter.
- **It is a synthetic voice.** If you publish the audio, say so.
- Long scripts may need splitting — audio models cap a single call.
