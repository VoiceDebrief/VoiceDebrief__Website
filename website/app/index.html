where do I find my voice note?

Laptop: open WhatsApp Web/Desktop → the voice message → download (.opus). Android: share/export the message (.opus/.ogg). iPhone: forwarding between apps produces .m4a — that works too.

🎙

Your audio and its transcript are sent to OpenRouter, which passes them to a model provider. We do not know which provider handles your recording, and we have no control over what they do with it. Do not use this for anything you would not hand to a third party.

The transcript always stays in the language spoken. The summary and infographic are built from the translation.

Written by a model, not a stenographer. It can mishear, and it can add words nobody said. Check anything that matters against the audio.
