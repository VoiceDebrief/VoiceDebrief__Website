The principle

Roles, not vibes

An agentic team without structure produces the same mess as a human team without structure — faster. The structure here is simple: each role has a written ROLE file (mandate, boundaries, outputs), a folder for its reviews, and the rulebook binds them all to the reality discipline. When a role has produced nothing, its empty folder says so — the structure is honest about its own exercise.
