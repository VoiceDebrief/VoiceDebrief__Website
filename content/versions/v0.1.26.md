version: v0.1.26
date: 2026-08-14
title: VoiceDebrief goes live — the whole pass on the home page, a Tools section, and audio out of any video
---

- The product is now **VoiceDebrief**, at **voicedebrief.ai**, with the QA copy at **qa.voicedebrief.ai** (issue 060) — a chat voice message is one of several ways a recording arrives here, so naming the product after one of them was describing the wrong thing
- The old `whatsapp-voice-transcription.sgraph.ai` host has **no DNS at all** — not a redirect, a failure to resolve — and 39 files were still naming it, including every `<link>` in the RSS feed, the sitemap, `robots.txt`, every canonical tag and the machine-readable files agents read. All swept onto the new address; the dated record was deliberately left alone, because rewriting history is not a link fix
- **You can run a recording straight from the home page** (issue 060): drop a voice note, choose whether you want it translated and whether you want an infographic, read the most it can cost, and press run. It is the same declared workflow the Workbench runs, from the same modules — the test proves it by matching the execution trace rather than the screen
- **See it work before you need a key** (issue 063): a *no key needed* button runs a complete pass in front of you — progress rows, transcript, debrief, cost accounting. It does not simulate the product, it runs it; only the model's answers are substituted, and it is stamped DEMO at the top of the panel. Its test aborts any network request rather than mocking it, so reaching OpenRouter fails the build instead of quietly costing someone money
- The infographic is deliberately **not** faked in the demo — a canned picture of somebody else's data would be the most misleading thing on the page — so that step degrades with a reason, which is also how the real product behaves when a step cannot run
- **You are no longer asked for a key before you have seen anything**: the key is requested when you press run, and the pass carries on by itself once it is saved. **[/openrouter-key/](/openrouter-key/)** takes someone from nothing to a working key in five steps, with *set a spend limit* called out as the one that matters
- The key panel now **collapses once a key is saved** — one quiet line with a *change* control instead of an empty form greeting people who had already done it. A rejected key reopens the form, because collapsing on failure hides the problem behind a tick
- **Thirteen claims on the site that were not true are fixed** (issue 060), and the three that mattered most were fixed by deleting something rather than rewording it: the **privacy mode chips are gone** (two of the three modes had never been built, and a selector promises a control we do not have), the **pricing claims are gone** (no plan, no credit, no card — you use your own key and we add nothing), and the phantom **"analysis" artefact is gone** from the hero, the diagram and the cards
- In place of the privacy chips is a plain statement of what actually happens — your audio and its transcript go to OpenRouter, which passes them to a model provider we neither choose nor know — placed where a file could still leave your device, and deliberately not dressed as a warning
- **The home page is the redesign** (issue 060): the panel sits beside the headline on a desktop and *first* on a phone, because the recording is the point; below it, how a recording gets out of six different apps and devices, and three real cost figures. `/app/` is now called the **Workbench** — its address has not changed
- **Five colour schemes, switchable from the header** — Signal (light, the default), Night, Paper, Blueprint and Ember, remembered per browser and painted before the page first appears. The Workbench stays light for now and the picker says why: nine of its older components still read colour through a compatibility layer where one name does two jobs
- **Colour is now one file**: `website/vd-tokens.css` holds all five schemes and repaints everything from them, including inside eight shadow roots. The green moved off Meta's `#25d366` to `#0E9E72` — same family, dark enough to be legible as text on white. "No colour arrives outside the tokens file" is now a build failure rather than a note
- **A Tools section** (issue 064): **[/tools/](/tools/)**, in the top menu, starting with **[text to speech](/tools/text-to-speech/)** — paste text, pick one of six voices, download the spoken `.wav`. One call to OpenRouter with your own key, and what it cost is read back from the generation afterwards rather than estimated. The voice pane that shipped on the Updates page the day before is retired into it, rather than left as a second copy
- **A video goes in, a transcript comes out** (issue 065): **[/tools/extract-audio/](/tools/extract-audio/)** pulls the soundtrack out of an `.mp4`, `.mov` or `.webm` as an `.m4a` and hands it to the app in one click. FFmpeg runs as WebAssembly **in your own tab**, so a 400 MB film is never uploaded — and it is the first thing on this site that costs nothing: no key, no model, no spend
- Screen recordings are handled honestly: they are Opus in WebM, which cannot be copied into an `.m4a`, and the underlying module reports that as *"the file may not contain an audio stream"* — which is false and sends people hunting for a fault in their file. The tool catches it, re-encodes and says so
- Fixed: **extracting audio was broken on the day it was published** (issue 065) — the browser refused to create FFmpeg's worker, so the 32 MB core was never even requested and nothing visible happened. Also fixed: the drop zone now gives way to the video you chose with a **Change** button, picking the same file twice in a row works, and the player and next-step buttons wait until there is a result to act on
- Fixed: **the infographic never drew on the home page** (issue 060) — the page handed the renderer a container that had never been attached to the document. It now draws into its own tab, which appears as soon as you ask for one rather than when the picture arrives, so the transcript and debrief are readable during the 60–90 second wait. It also fits the pane it is drawn in, which it did not when a model returned something 1024px wide
- **A running pass shows its work and can be stopped** (issue 060): the progress list renders the pass's own trace, so every step says what became of it — done, skipped or stopped — instead of advancing by guesswork past a step that was skipped or failed. Each artefact appears the moment it exists, the step in progress carries a running clock, and every finished run leaves a folded **What ran** record that opens itself when something did not go cleanly
- The **Library menu opens pages again**: three of its five entries were home-page fragments, so *How it works* scrolled the page you were already on. Each now goes to the page that owns the subject, and a test fails the build if a menu entry names a page that is not on disk
- **[/engineering/wasm/](/engineering/wasm/)** explains how the browser does the work — the nine steps from pressing *Extract* to the `.m4a`, why WebAssembly is what lets a product with no backend do real work, and what it costs: 32 MB once, the tab's memory, one core rather than all of them, and a CDN inside the security policy. It states plainly that **transcription is not local**
- Tools publish their agent-facing API **synchronously** (issue 064) — an external diagnostic found `window.__tool` undefined after a normal load, and it was right: publication waited on a second origin, which took 1860 ms when that origin was reachable and never when it was blocked. The rule this leaves behind: an agent-facing API must be at least as available as the UI beside it
- Internal: the public record had drifted three releases behind, so two gates now make that impossible to repeat quietly — the release named by the `version` file must be published on [/versions/](/versions/), and every published version must have its changelog heading
