title: Go-live — the home page redesign, a Tools section, and every false claim removed
---

- The home page is the redesign (issue 060): the workflow runs beside the headline on desktop and first on a phone, five colour schemes (two of them dark) are switchable from the header, and a running pass now shows its own trace with a **Stop this pass** button instead of a step that spins with no way out
- **Go live honestly**: an audit found thirteen claims on the site that were not true, and all thirteen are fixed — mostly by deleting rather than rewording. The privacy-mode selector is gone (two of its three modes were never built) in favour of a plain statement of where your audio actually goes; every invented pricing claim is gone; so is the phantom "analysis" artefact and language like "in seconds" and "every time"
- You are no longer asked for an OpenRouter key before you have seen anything — a stranger can load a recording, choose translation and an infographic, and read the quoted maximum before being asked to sign in; **[/openrouter-key/](/openrouter-key/)** gets you a working key in five steps
- A **Tools section** ([/tools/](/tools/)): small single-purpose browser tools built alongside the app. Text to speech (issue 064) turns any text into a spoken `.wav` on your own OpenRouter key, and Extract audio from a video (issue 065) pulls the soundtrack out of an `.mp4`/`.mov`/`.webm` entirely as WebAssembly in your tab — free, nothing uploaded, nothing to upload it to. Both publish a JS API (`window.__tool`) so an agent can drive them without a UI, and extract-audio hands its result straight to the app with one click
- **An agent could not find that API, and it was right** (issue 064): it used to wait on a second origin before publishing anything, so it was undefined for up to 1860 ms on a good path and forever when that origin was blocked — the normal case for a sandboxed agent browser. It now publishes synchronously during module evaluation, and `window.__toolStatus` always states its readiness rather than leaving it to a missed console warning
- **VoiceDebrief** is the name everywhere the site is served, and the domain move is complete — 39 files that still pointed at the old host, including every link in the RSS feed, now resolve
- A demo path needs no key and no account: one button runs the real production pass — same declared workflow, same trace, same budget accounting — with only the model answers substituted, so it cannot drift from what the product actually does
- The Library menu goes to real pages instead of scrolling the page you were already on, and a new page, **[/engineering/wasm/](/engineering/wasm/)**, explains how the extract-audio tool does real work with no backend
- The public record catches up with itself: three releases (v0.1.23–v0.1.25) had shipped without a CHANGELOG heading or a `/versions/` entry, and two new gates make that drift impossible to repeat quietly
- Internal: the workflow state machine gains a `facts` namespace, and CSP hardening closes a blob-worker gap that had silently stopped the extract-audio tool from ever fetching FFmpeg
